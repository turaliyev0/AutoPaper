# Stability of Long-term Neural Implants
This study investigates the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) degradation in chronically implanted microelectrode arrays. We hypothesize that a polyimide coating reduces gliosis-induced impedance spikes by 15% over a 6-month period compared to uncoated silicon probes.
Keywords: neural engineering; biocompatibility; SNR; microelectrodes.
