================================================================================
EXPERIMENTAL PARAMETERS AND METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

SUBJECT MODEL:
- Species: Sprague-Dawley rats (n=12)
- Age: 8-10 weeks
- Weight: 250-300g

IMPLANT CONFIGURATION (Hemispheric Control Design):
- CONTROL GROUP (Left Hemisphere): Rigid Silicon Probes (Standard Utah-style geometry).
- TEST GROUP (Right Hemisphere): Polyimide-coated Flexible Thin-film Probes.
- Channel Count: 32 channels per hemisphere.

MECHANICAL PROPERTIES:
- Silicon Probe Young's Modulus: ~180 GPa.
- Polyimide Probe Young's Modulus: ~3 GPa.
- Brain Tissue Stiffness Reference: ~1-30 kPa.

MATERIAL AND COATING SPECIFICATIONS:
- Substrate Material: Polyimide (PI-2611) with 50µm total thickness.
- Conductive Sites: Gold (Au) micro-electrodes with PEDOT:PSS electrochemical coating.
- Coating Purpose: To lower 1 kHz impedance and increase the effective surface area for charge injection.

DATA ACQUISITION AND SIGNAL PROCESSING:
- Sampling Frequency: 30 kHz per channel.
- Hardware: Intan Technologies RHD2000 series.
- Filtering: Digital 500 Hz high-pass filter applied for spike detection.
- Monitoring Frequency: Daily recording sessions (30 mins each) under resting state.

METRICS FOR ANALYSIS:
1. Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR): Defined as V_peak-to-peak / (2 * RMS_noise).
2. Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS): Weekly sweeps from 10 Hz to 100 kHz.
3. Histological Analysis: Post-mortem slice staining (GFAP and NeuN) to quantify glial scarring and neuronal density at the 180-day mark.

PROCEDURE NOTES:
- Implantation via stereotaxic surgery under isoflurane anesthesia.
- Post-operative care included 7 days of antibiotics and analgesics.
- Calibration of impedance spectroscopy performed daily at 1 kHz to ensure electrode integrity.
